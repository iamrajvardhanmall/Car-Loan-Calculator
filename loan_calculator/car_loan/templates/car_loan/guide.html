Your toolkit

What each feature does

Dashboard / Loan Calculator

Estimate monthly payment and total cost using vehicle price, loan terms, and optional ownership costs such as insurance, fuel, maintenance, and warranty.

Saved Calculations

Save a scenario to your account so you can return to it while comparing vehicles, rates, or down-payment amounts.

Compare Loans

Create multiple loan scenarios and compare monthly payments and overall costs. Do not focus only on the lowest monthly payment.

Value Estimator

Enter make, model, year, mileage, condition, and location for an indicative vehicle-value estimate. It is not a guaranteed market or trade-in price.

Budget Analyzer

Review income, existing debts, and expenses to understand how a potential payment may fit within your monthly budget.

Results and PDF summary

Review the result summary and download a PDF when you need a convenient record for your own planning.

Plain-language glossary

Important terminology

These definitions explain the labels used throughout the app.

Vehicle price

The listed or agreed purchase price before financing costs and other fees.

Down payment

The amount paid upfront. A larger down payment usually reduces the amount borrowed.

Trade-in value

The estimated value of a vehicle given to the dealer, which may reduce the amount financed.

Loan amount / principal

The amount borrowed after relevant upfront contributions and adjustments.

Loan term

The length of the loan in months. A longer term can lower the monthly payment but increase total interest.

Interest rate

The annual percentage charged by the lender for borrowing money.

Monthly payment

The estimated regular payment toward the loan, excluding ownership costs you have not entered.

Total repayment

The estimated amount paid over the full term, including principal and calculated interest.

Total interest

The estimated cost of borrowing over the loan term, separate from the amount borrowed.

Sales tax

A tax applied to the purchase, entered as a percentage. Actual rules depend on location.

Documentation and registration fees

Charges for processing paperwork and registering the vehicle. Confirm actual values with the seller or authority.

Loan origination fee

A charge for arranging the loan, commonly entered as a percentage or included in financing costs.

Credit score

A number lenders use to assess credit risk. It does not determine approval inside this app.

Debt-to-income ratio (DTI)

The share of monthly income used for debt payments. A lower DTI generally leaves more room in a budget.

Mileage and condition

Vehicle inputs used by the value estimator. Market prices can still vary significantly.

Important reminder

Auto Concept provides estimates for planning and education. It does not approve loans, guarantee vehicle values, or replace qualified financial advice. Confirm rates, taxes, fees, insurance, and final loan documents with the relevant provider.
