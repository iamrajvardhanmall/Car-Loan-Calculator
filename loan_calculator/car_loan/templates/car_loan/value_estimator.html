How to Use This Estimator

Manual Input Method:

  1. Select your car's Make and Model
  2. Enter the Year (1990-2026)
  3. Input the Mileage (any positive number)
  4. Select the vehicle Condition
  5. Check any relevant Features
  6. Click "Estimate Value"

Image Analysis Method:

  1. Upload multiple photos of your car (front, side, rear, and interior)
  2. Optionally provide Mileage and Year
  3. Click "Estimate from Images"

Tips for Best Results:

  • For manual input, provide accurate details
  • For image analysis, upload clear photos from multiple angles
  • Include photos of the exterior, interior, and any damage
  • Show the license plate and VIN if possible
